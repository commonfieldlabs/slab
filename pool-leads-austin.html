How SLAB works

Every Friday SLAB pulls the City of Austin's newly issued building permits, keeps the new single-family homes, removes duplicates and maps them. Subscribers get the map plus a spreadsheet for their ZIP codes: street address, size, builder and estimated move-in month. That's enough to plan mailers, door hangers or a builder pitch before the house is finished.

Questions

Where does the data come from?
From the City of Austin's open data portal (Issued Construction Permits, dataset 3syk-w9eu). SLAB keeps new single-family and duplex building permits, removes duplicates and maps each home using the city's address points.


How early do I hear about a new home?
The building permit is the first public record of a new house. Families typically move in about 7 months after the permit is issued, so SLAB gives you months of lead time. Move-in month is an estimate (permit date + 7 months).


How is this different from a new mover list?
New mover lists come out after people move in, when every other company is already mailing them. SLAB starts at the permit, months before move-in.
