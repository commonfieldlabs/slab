Questions

What is SLAB?
SLAB is a weekly list of every new house that got a building permit in Austin, TX, for companies that sell to new homeowners: pools, solar, landscaping, fencing, security, window coverings, gutters, water treatment, pest control, movers, cleaning and home insurance.


Where does the data come from?
From the City of Austin's open data portal (Issued Construction Permits, dataset 3syk-w9eu). SLAB keeps new single-family and duplex building permits, removes duplicates and maps each home using the city's address points.


How early do I hear about a new home?
The building permit is the first public record of a new house. Families typically move in about 7 months after the permit is issued, so SLAB gives you months of lead time. Move-in month is an estimate (permit date + 7 months).


How is this different from a new mover list?
New mover lists come out after people move in, when every other company is already mailing them. SLAB starts at the permit, months before move-in.


What does it cost?
$49 a month for every new home in the ZIP codes you serve, delivered every Friday. Or a one-time Territory Report for $750: 12 months of new homes in your service area, mapped and ranked, with builders and a move-in calendar.


What's in each record?
Street address, ZIP code, square footage, builder, permit date, estimated move-in month and a link to the city's permit record. No homeowner names, phone numbers or emails.


What area does it cover?
Permits issued by the City of Austin, Texas. Surrounding cities with their own permit offices are not included yet.
